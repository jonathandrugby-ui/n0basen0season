The Programme

Full Gym. Rugby Basics. Five Weeks.

Two sessions a week. Thursday is the gym. Saturday is the gym and the ball. Every lift is coached from the ground up and scaled to where you are.

In the gym
  • Squat, hinge, press and pull, taught from empty bar up.
  • Trunk and neck strength for contact.
  • Sprint mechanics, change of direction and landing.
  • Conditioning that looks like a rugby match, not a treadmill.
On the ball
  • Catch and pass under pressure, both hands.
  • Body height, footwork and safe tackle technique.
  • Breakdown basics: ball presentation and clean out.
  • Game understanding you can carry into any position.

The 5 weeks

Outline is provisional until confirmed by the head coach.
  1. Week 1Screen and foundationsMovement screen, baseline tests, bar skills.
  2. Week 2Strength baseLoad the big lifts. Catch and pass.
  3. Week 3Power and contactJumps, throws, tackle technique.
  4. Week 4Speed and conditioningSprints, repeat efforts, breakdown work.
  5. Week 5Test and carry it forwardRetest, review, your off season plan.

Parents

Straight Answers.

What parents ask before they sign a 13 to 19 year old up for a gym and rugby block.

Who is it for?
Players aged 13 to 19. Any position, any club or school, any level. If your child wants to play in 2027, this is the base.
Do they need gym experience?
No. Every lift starts with an empty bar and is coached one rep at a time. Loads are set per player, never per group.
Is it safe?
The head coach is BokSmart accredited and trained in first aid and CPR. 15 players, one coach, one floor. Tackle work is technique first, at walking pace before anything else.
What do they bring?
Trainers, boots for Saturday, a water bottle and a towel. A gumguard for contact work. Everything else is at the gym.
What if they miss a session?
Tell the coach on WhatsApp before the session. The programme is built on 10 sessions, so missed work is caught up where the coach can fit it in. There are no refunds for missed sessions.
How do I pay?
R1 500 via PayFast, straight after the sign up form. A spot is held once payment clears. Parents sign the indemnity in the form.
