    Players, this part is for you

Start before 5 Nov.

Nothing here needs a gym. Do it between now and the first session.

Sort your kit. Running or gym training shoes, a water bottle and a towel. No boots for now. I’ll tell you when to bring them, and a gumguard, before contact work starts. Everything else is at the gym.
Move every day. A jog, a swim, a game of touch, a walk with the dog. Nothing heavy. Arrive with a body that’s been used.
Get a ball in your hands. Catch and pass with anyone who’ll stand opposite you. Both hands. Weak hand twice as much.
Sleep and drink water. 8 to 10 hours a night. Drink water through the day. Thirsty at training is too late.
Come fresh on Thursday. Week 1 is a movement screen and baseline tests. There’s nothing to train for. I need to see where you actually are, then we build from there.
Parents
Every session is running or gym training shoes only, Thursdays and Saturdays. Boots only when I ask for them. If anything medical changes between now and 5 Nov, WhatsApp me before the first session.
