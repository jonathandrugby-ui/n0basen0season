Gmail

  1. Settings (the gear) > See all settings > General > Signature > Create new. Name it "Coach".
  2. Click in the box and paste. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
  3. Set it as the default for new emails and replies, and save again.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Mail > Settings > Signatures. Pick your account and click +.
  2. Untick "Always match my default message font", then paste into the right hand box.

iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page on the iPhone, press Copy signature.
  2. Settings > Apps > Mail > Signature, paste, then shake the phone and choose Undo Change Attributes if the styling drops out.

Outlook

  1. Settings > Mail (or Accounts) > Signatures > New signature.
  2. Paste, name it "Coach", choose it for new messages and replies, and save.
