What the Form Asks.

Have the player next to you. Medical notes and the indemnity need a parent or guardian.

  • 01Player name and date of birth
  • 02School, club and playing position
  • 03Parent or guardian name, WhatsApp number and email
  • 04Medical conditions, injuries and medication
  • 05Emergency contact
  • 06Indemnity and consent tick, by the parent or guardian
  • 07Photo consent tick, optional
