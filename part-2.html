No Base. No Season. Part 2.

Who it is for

U12 to U19. Back for more, or brand new.

Open to players from U12 to U19. That includes players returning from the first 5 weeks and new recruits. Every player is tested against the No Base. No Season. norms for their age band: 12 to 14, and 15 to 19.

What it builds

A formidable base: strength, speed, endurance, confidence, mental capacity and ability, plus the skills that lift players on the field.

Group and individual

Group sessions build the collective: shared standards, skills and conditioning. Individual programmes are built from each player's baseline results and age band, so every player works on what moves them forward most.

Starting point

Baseline testing.

Returning players carry their results over from the first 5 weeks. New recruits complete the full baseline battery in week 1.

Physical tests
  • Trap bar deadlift
  • Sit ups
  • Medicine ball chest throw
  • 30 m sprint
  • 5 10 5 agility
  • Assault bike 1000 m
Movement scores

Bodyweight squat, kettlebell swing, bodyweight lunge, push up and single leg balance.

Skill scores

Gym, catching and passing, tackling, rucking and positional.

The 5 weeks

From athlete to rugby player.

After testing, the training is programmed in building blocks that move players from athletic foundations to rugby specific work.

  1. Week 1Test and foundationsBaseline testing for new recruits. Results carried over for returning players. Movement quality and technique foundations.
  2. Week 2Build the baseStrength foundations, speed mechanics, aerobic base and core rugby skills.
  3. Week 3Load and powerContact skills (tackling and rucking), agility and conditioning. Lighter loading around the Christmas back to back days.
  4. Week 4Rugby specificGame speed skills, positional work, decision making under fatigue and mental capacity.
  5. Week 5ConsolidateRetest against the norms, individual feedback and next steps for each player.
The weekly focus is a working draft and can change as the programme is finalised.
